रीति · RitiBackup

Intelligent encrypted backup control for Home Assistant. v1.1.0.

Backups are sealed on-device with ChaCha20-Poly1305 (when a passphrase is set) and replicated to your configured WebDAV, S3, and Backblaze B2 backends. Configure backends and the encryption passphrase in the add-on's Configuration tab.
